## 3. Standard Feature Bullet Points
- ⚡ **100% In-Browser Processing:** Executes via FFmpeg core in WebAssembly with multi-threaded SharedArrayBuffer acceleration.
- 🔒 **Zero Cloud Uploads:** Video bytes never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy for personal and confidential footage.
- 🎯 **3 Intelligent Framing Modes:** Focal-point crop, ambient blurred background extension, and clean letterbox / pillarbox bars.
- 🔊 **Direct Audio Stream Copy:** Preserves original AAC/MP3 audio streams without generational compression loss.
- 🚫 **No Watermarks & No Paywalls:** Completely free with no account creation required.
